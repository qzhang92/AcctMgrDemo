DISCOVERY BEFORE PROPOSAL

Build the case with the customer.

  • Confirm the population

    Which teams need additional managed seats? Are they new users, a phased rollout, or a temporary capacity requirement?

  • Validate readiness

    Who owns onboarding, adoption, support routing, and the service handover?

  • Agree timing

    What effective date is required? Document any proration instead of assuming a full year.

  • Separate the approval path

    Use a signed change order. Do not silently roll the option into the like-for-like base renewal.
